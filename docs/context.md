## Architecture

**Entry chain.** `App/StoreSimApp.swift` (`@main`) opens a `WindowGroup` with
`StoreView`. `StoreView` builds the scene once: `SceneComposer.compose(layout:detail:catalogue:)`
(pure, in `StoreSimKit`) returns a platform-neutral `SceneNode` tree, and
`EntityBuilder.entity(for:loader:)` (in `StoreSimRender`) turns it into RealityKit
entities. `CameraController` (`App/Camera/`) owns the `OrbitCamera` and the
selection; HUD cards live in `App/HUD/HUDCards.swift`.

**Two package targets, one purity rule.** `StoreSimKit` imports only Foundation
and simd (lint rule b), so everything about the layout, the fixtures, the camera
maths and the scene tree is testable without a GPU. `StoreSimRender` is the only
place RealityKit, CoreGraphics images and Metal appear.

**Layout is data.** `StoreLayout.plumCorner` lists every fixture on a 0.5 m grid
(a 56 x 42 cell lot: 28 m x 21 m). `LayoutValidator` refuses overlaps, fixtures
outside the lot or in the wrong zone, unreachable interior cells and aisles
narrower than one metre. Ceiling or wall-line kinds (`pendantLamp`, `menuScreen`,
`windowPoster`, `entranceDoor`) take no floor cells (`FixtureKind.occupiesFloor`).

**Fixtures are composed builders.** Every `FixtureKind` has a builder in
`Scene/Builders/` that assembles many named sub-parts and honours `DetailLevel`
(`.full` on the Mac, `.reduced` on the phone). `FixtureBuilders.minimumPartCount`
is the floor the tests enforce. Product rows are seeded from the fixture id with
a hash of our own, never `hashValue`.

**Asset catalogue seam.** `AssetCatalogue` keeps `.usdz` files whose basename
equals an `AssetName` raw value; the app builds it from the `Models` folder
reference (`App/Resources/Models`, `type: folder` in `project.yml`). A fixture
with a model becomes `.asset(name)` and keeps its procedural tree as a child
named `fallback`, used when loading throws. `ModelLoader` is the only file that
loads a model (lint rule g). The brief for the artist is `docs/asset-brief.md`.

**Per-instance variety (2026-10-17, plan `plans/2026-10-17-per-instance-model-variety.md`).**
`ModelVariant` (pure) derives a variant index for each placed fixture: its ordinal
among fixtures of the same kind in `layout.fixtures`, wrapped at the kind's variant
count (3 for posters, shelving and fridges, 1 otherwise). It also holds the poster
headlines and tints and the seven-colour product window per variant. The index
travels on `SceneNode.variant`; the stand-in builders take it as `variant:`, and
`ModelVariants.apply` (in `StoreSimRender`) honours it right after the one
`Entity.load` in `ModelLoader` (poster texture offset, product recolour). USD
variant sets were rejected: RealityKit offers no public way to select a variant
after loading, and one file per variant would break the one-file-per-`AssetName`
catalogue.

**Camera and roof.** `OrbitCamera` is a pure value: pan, zoom, rotate and tilt
are clamped, and `roofOpacity` is 1 below 30 degrees of pitch, 0 above 50,
linear between. `EntityBuilder.applyRoofOpacity` sets the `store_roof`
`OpacityComponent` on every camera change. The roof carries no collision shape,
so it never swallows a click.

**Name tag.** The floating tag is an in-scene entity (`NameTagEntity`), because
`RealityView` attachments are not available on macOS or iOS. Tried and not
available: attachments (closure takes one argument) and
`.realityViewCameraControls(.orbit)` (exposes no camera state, so the roof fade
could not follow it), hence the hand-rolled orbit camera.

**Headless snapshots.** `OffscreenRenderer` wraps `RealityRenderer` and renders
into a Metal texture without a window; `SnapshotRenderTests` writes two PNGs
under `NSTemporaryDirectory()/storesim-snapshots/` and skips only when no GPU
exists.

**Asset generation.** `Tools/assetgen` (Python, `usd-core` and `Pillow`, pinned
by hash) builds the twenty-nine assets. `Scripts/models.sh` writes them into
`App/Resources/Models/`. The catalogue resolves `.usdz` for fixtures and `.png`
for signage; `ModelLoader` is the only file that loads either. Decision
2026-10-17, plan `plans/2026-10-17-grok-models-from-asset-brief.md`: generate
from the brief so CI can prove the shipped files match the generator
byte-for-byte, instead of checking in a model that cannot be rebuilt.

**Decisions (2026-10-17, plan `plans/2026-10-17-3d-store-and-surroundings.md`).**
- XcodeGen over a hand-kept `.xcodeproj` (a pbxproj is opaque and merge-hostile;
  `project.yml` is readable), over Tuist (a Swift manifest toolchain for no gain
  at this size) and over a package-only app (SwiftPM cannot produce an iOS app
  bundle). The generated project is gitignored; generation is the first line of
  the build gate, so a fresh checkout works.
- `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` was deleted: it deployed a web app to a hosting
  service with a database branch per pull request, and this project has no web
  surface. The `/deploy-web` skill stays in the pack (managed) and does not apply here.
- The web preflight profile (`.claude/skills/ship/PREFLIGHT-web.md`) is part of the
  managed pack and is left in place; its keyword checks are harmless for a Swift
  plan. A Swift profile is a follow-up card.
- CI runs on `macos-26` (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`); the runner's Xcode may lag
  the local Xcode 26.6 the project was built with.
- The lot is 56 x 42 cells (the plan said 56 x 36, which cannot hold a 2-cell
  facade strip, a 14-cell shop, a 6-cell sidewalk, a 4-cell parking lane, a
  12-cell street and a 4-cell far sidewalk).

## Conventions

The project's own non-negotiables. Each is enforced by a preflight check, a
verifier grep or a domain reviewer; a plan that breaks one is a plan that will
not pass. The planner reads these before writing, the implementer before
coding.

1. **Brand single source.** `Brand/Brand.swift` is the only file with a colour
   literal or the brand name. *(lint gate c and d)*
2. **`StoreSimKit` imports only Foundation and simd.** No SwiftUI, RealityKit,
   AppKit or UIKit in the pure target. *(lint gate b)*
3. **Layout is data.** Fixtures live in `StoreLayout`; builders never position
   fixtures; the shipped layout validates clean. *(`DefaultLayoutTests`)*
4. **Every fixture is a composed builder with named sub-parts** and honours
   `DetailLevel`. *(`FixtureDetailTests`)*
5. **Both shells, same pass.** A screen change states what desktop and mobile
   each do and branches in code (`horizontalSizeClass`, `#if os(...)`), never a
   duplicate view tree. *(preflight BLOCK, integration reviewer)*
6. **Models and textures load only through `AssetCatalogue` and `ModelLoader`.**
   *(lint gate g)*
7. **No SceneKit.** *(lint gate a)*
8. **English-only sources, no competitor names or trade dress.**
   *(lint gate e and f)*
9. **Persisted shapes are forward-compatible.** Every `Codable` gets defaults on
   read, removed keys are tolerated, migrations are additive.
   *(`DefaultLayoutTests` decoding case)*
10. **Metres, Y up, fixture pivot at the bottom centre of its footprint, +Z is
    the fixture's front.** *(asset brief, integration reviewer)*
11. **Never commit or push** unless explicitly asked.
12. **Shipped models are generated.** `App/Resources/Models/` is never hand-edited;
    `bash Scripts/models.sh --check` in CI proves the committed files equal the
    generator's output. The generator reads colours from the brief's palette
    table and the name from `Brand.swift`. *(lint gates d and h)*
13. **Variety is derived, never stored.** `ModelVariant.index(of:in:)` is the only source of a variant index; it lives on `SceneNode.variant` and is applied by `ModelVariants.apply`; no `Codable` type carries it. *(`ModelVariantTests`, `SceneComposerTests`; verifier grep on `Fixture.swift` and `StoreLayout.swift`)*

## What the tests cannot see

The suite is hermetic. Green proves nothing about these surfaces, which is why
the domain reviewers and the audit skill exist:

- **How it looks and feels on a real screen.** Colour balance, gesture speed and
  frame rate on a phone are judgements; the snapshot test only proves a real
  picture comes out.
- **The signed, archived app.** CI and local builds are ad-hoc signed.
- **The Xcode version on the CI runner.** It may lag the local one.
- **How a generated model reads on a real screen next to the brief.** Size,
  pivot, facing, triangle budget, file presence, fallback, pixel distance and
  `usdchecker` are asserted. Whether it looks like the shop in the brief is a
  judgement on a Mac screen.
